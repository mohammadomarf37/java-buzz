Our Story

Founded in 2005 by Chef Antonio Rossi, Café Aylanto brings the rich culinary traditions of the Mediterranean to life. Our philosophy centers on using only the freshest local ingredients combined with time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

Every dish is crafted with passion and precision, reflecting the vibrant cultures of Greece, Italy, Spain, and the coastal regions of North Africa. We invite you to experience a culinary journey that celebrates the essence of Mediterranean hospitality.
